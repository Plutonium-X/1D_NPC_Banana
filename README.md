  Banana! The one and only banana Fighter/Mage/Thief!

She can be found near the entrance to the Copper Coronet.

**Features:**

Ability scores: STR 18/00, DEX 18, CON 18, INT 18, WIS 18, CHA 18. If that bothers you, there's <a href="https://github.com/Deratiseur/Mods_Tweaker">a mod</a> to fix it.

Her dialogue consists entirely of her introduction.

She is fully voiced (in English).

She has 100% vulnerability to crushing damage.
